Our Purpose

Why We Built EV Disha

Choosing an electric vehicle in India involves evaluating unfamiliar specifications—kilowatt-hour battery capacities, LFP versus NMC cell chemistries, MIDC and IDC range test cycles, CCS2 versus portable AC charging, and multi-year battery warranties.

EV Disha ("Disha" meaning direction) brings electric cars, scooters, motorcycles, and commercial three- and four-wheelers together into a fast, distraction-free catalogue where specifications are standardized for side-by-side comparison.

Transparency & Accuracy

Editorial Specification & Verification Policy

EV Disha is designed as a fast, privacy-respecting electric mobility discovery platform—with zero sign-up walls, zero ad clutter, and instant browser-side vehicle comparison across all five EV segments.

All vehicle records, battery parameters, driving range ratings, and indicative ex-showroom prices are curated from published manufacturer brochures and official launch disclosures. Because ex-showroom prices, state EV road-tax waivers, PM E-DRIVE / state subsidies, and variant features vary by city and update frequently, buyers should always confirm final on-road quotes and specifications with an authorized dealership or brand sales team prior to booking.
