About EV Disha

Clarity for Every Electric Journey

EV Disha ("Disha" meaning direction) is an independent Indian electric mobility guide uniting 187+ electric cars, scooters, motorcycles, 3-wheelers, and commercial EVs with real India charging infrastructure, green loan EMI, and fuel-savings tools.

Zero sign-up walls, zero ad clutter, and standardized manufacturer & BIS IS 17017 charging data—built so buyers can evaluate specs transparently before visiting an authorized dealership.
