2. Indicative Prices, Subsidies & On-Road Costs

All prices shown across vehicle cards, detail pages, comparison tables, and AI assistant responses are indicative ex-showroom figures only. Actual retail and on-road prices vary substantially depending on:

  • Your city, state RTO registration charges, green tax / road-tax waivers, and municipal levies.
  • Eligibility and live applicability of central or state EV incentives (such as PM E-DRIVE or state EV policies).
  • Mandatory and optional motor insurance add-ons (Zero Depreciation, Battery Protect, Return-to-Invoice).
  • Home portable charger, AC wallbox hardware, standard cable length, and installation charges, which may be billed separately depending on variant.

Always request an official itemized pro-forma invoice from an authorized dealer or brand sales representative for accurate pricing clarity.

3. Specifications, Claimed Range, Battery & Features

Technical parameters—including battery kilowatt-hour (kWh) capacity, certified driving range (MIDC, ARAI, IDC, or Pedal-Assist), motor output, top speed, charging times, connector standards, variants, and equipment lists—are compiled from publicly available manufacturer disclosures for buyer comparison.

  • Real-World Range Variation: Certified test-cycle range is achieved under controlled laboratory conditions. Actual driving range varies with traffic, payload, gradient, tyre pressure, riding/driving mode, ambient temperature, AC usage, and battery age.
  • Manufacturer Revisions: OEMs reserve the right to modify specifications, add or remove features, revise warranty coverage, or discontinue variants/colours at any time without prior notice.

They do not constitute financial, tax, legal, or technical advice. Users must verify all figures directly with the respective OEM sales team, dealership, or financial institution.
